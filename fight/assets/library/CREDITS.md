# Library clips

These movieclips come from the StykAnimate community library (https://stykanimate.com/library/). Their makers keep ownership, and the library's terms let anyone use them in their animations. `node fight/bake-clips.mjs` turns them into the sprite strips in `../sprites`.

| File | Clip | By | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `lightning-effect.stykmov` | Lightning Effect | daniel_park | Red crackle around a powered-up aura |
| `rush-boost-clip.stykmov` | Rush Boost clip | Mr.Legend3x | Thrust behind dashes |
| `ground-dust-by-vamz.stykmov` | Ground Dust | Vamz | Shockwave dust on slams and landings |
