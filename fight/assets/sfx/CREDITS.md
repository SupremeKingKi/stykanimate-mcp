# Sound effects

## Kenney.nl (CC0)

`impact-sounds`, `rpg-audio` and `sci-fi-sounds` are Kenney's Impact Sounds, RPG Audio and Sci-fi Sounds packs ([kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl/)), released under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No credit is required. Each folder keeps Kenney's own licence file.
